What Minco offers

Support that feels human

No medical jargon. Just people who understand, and tools that keep it safe.

Community discussions

Join gentle, topic-based conversations about stress, sleep, motivation, and everyday life.

Peer support

Give and receive encouragement from people with lived experience — not professionals, but peers.

Safe space
