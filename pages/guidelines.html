Kind by design

Safe space guidelines

Minco works because we protect it together. Six simple promises keep every conversation supportive — especially on hard days.

1. Lead with kindness

No judgement, no mocking, no pile-ons. If you wouldn't say it gently to a friend, rephrase it — or just send support.

2. No medical advice

Share lived experience, not diagnoses or prescriptions. "What helped me" is welcome. "You should take..." is not.

3. Respect anonymity

Never ask for real names, locations, or contact details. Screenshots of private posts outside Minco are never okay.

4. Mark heavy topics

Add a content note (e.g. "grief", "panic") at the top of intense posts and images so others can choose when to read.

5. No crisis counselling

Minco is peer support, not emergency care. If someone may be unsafe, point them to local emergency help and report the post.

6. Report, don't retaliate

See harm? Report it and step away. Moderators review quickly. Revenge replies only make the space colder.

Do this

  • "That sounds really heavy. I'm here."
  • "Sleep has been hard for me too — want ideas?"
  • Upvote kind, helpful replies
  • Share small wins — they inspire others

Not this

  • "Just get over it" / "Others have it worse"
  • "You have depression, take X"
  • Demanding personal info or DMs
  • Graphic details without a content note

How reporting works

Three taps and a human reviews it. No drama, no public call-outs.

  1. 1. Tap Share → Report on any post or reply that breaks these guidelines.
  2. 2. Pick a reason — unkind, medical advice, privacy break, or unsafe content.
  3. 3. We review fast — harmful content is hidden, repeat harm loses posting access.
Crisis? Minco can't respond to emergencies. Please call your local emergency number or crisis helpline immediately. You deserve real-time help.
